Guided Medina Tour

The Medina through the eyes of someone who truly knows it, not a tourist trail.

Book this tour All experiences
The tour

Not a workshop, but the perfect way to begin

Most visitors see the Medina’s main streets and souvenir stalls. We open the doors behind them: quiet courtyards, working ateliers and the corners locals actually use, told through the stories of the people who live and make there.

It is an easy, unhurried morning walk and a natural companion to the craft workshops, the wider world the crafts come from.

What you’ll experience

The Medina, off the trail

No. 1

Hidden corners

Tucked-away passages, rooftops and courtyards you would walk straight past without a guide who grew up with them.

No. 2

Living craftsmanship

Real ateliers still at work, where you see the crafts in their everyday setting, not a staged display.

No. 3

Stories, not scripts

History, daily life and small human details, shared by someone who truly knows the place.

How it flows

Your morning, step by step

1 · Meet at the gate

We gather at a set meeting point at one of the Medina’s historic gates, shared with you when you book.

2 · Walk the inner Medina

Through the souks and side lanes to the ateliers and hidden corners, stopping wherever a story or a workshop is worth it.

3 · End with your bearings

We finish near the heart of the Medina, so you can carry on exploring, or sit down for a mint tea, on your own.

The details

Ready to see the real Medina?

About 1.5 hours on foot, Saturdays & Sundays from 9:00. Small group, led in English and French. Meeting point shared on booking. 39 € per person.
