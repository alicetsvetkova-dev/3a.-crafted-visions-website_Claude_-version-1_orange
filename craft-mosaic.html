The craft

An art Tunisia has practiced since antiquity

Mosaic is one of the oldest crafts in the country: the Roman and Byzantine floors of Tunisia are among the finest anywhere, and the tradition never fully left. Guided by a mosaicist, you learn to cut, place and set each tessera, stone and ceramic, into a composition of your own.

It is patient, absorbing work where a picture appears one small piece at a time. You leave with a finished mosaic that is yours to keep.

Why it’s good for you

Focus you can hold in your hands

No. 1

Deep, quiet focus

Placing each piece asks for full attention, the kind that quietly pushes everything else out of the room and settles the mind.

No. 2

Colour and composition

Choosing tones and arranging fragments trains the eye and rewards patience: small decisions that add up to a whole.

No. 3

Something that lasts

A mosaic is durable by nature. You walk away with a solid, finished object, and a real sense of accomplishment.

The session

Ready to set your first tesserae?

About 2 hours in a mosaic atelier in Tunis. Small group, maximum 6 people. All materials included, no experience needed. In French. 49 € per person, and your mosaic comes home with you.
