Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This policy explains what personal data we process when you visit this website or contact us, and the rights you have. The controller responsible for processing is:

Crafted Visions Ltd.
22 str. Svilenitsa, 1463 Sofia, Bulgaria
Email: crafted.visions@outlook.com · Phone: +216 51 639 199

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, your browser transmits technical data (including your IP address, browser type and time of access) to the host, which processes it in server log files to deliver the site securely. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a stable, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Fonts

Pages load the “Spectral” and “Hanken Grotesk” typefaces from Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd.). When a font is requested, your IP address is transmitted to Google. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in presenting the site consistently (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Contact & inquiry forms

When you use the contact or team-building inquiry form, the data you enter (such as name, email, company, and your message) is transmitted through our forms processor Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA) and delivered to us by email. We use it solely to respond to your request. Legal basis: steps prior to entering a contract and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to handle your request and to meet any legal retention periods.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, your email address is processed by our newsletter provider Mailchimp (Intuit Inc., USA) to send you our updates. Subscription uses a confirmation (double opt-in) step; you can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Bookings & payment

Workshop bookings are completed through Stripe (Stripe, Inc. / Stripe Payments Europe Ltd.). Payment and billing details are entered on Stripe’s checkout and processed by Stripe as an independent controller/processor under its own privacy policy; we do not receive or store your full card details. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Links to social platforms

Our site links to Instagram and WhatsApp. We do not embed their tracking on our pages; their data practices apply only once you follow a link and interact with those services.

International transfers

Several providers above (GitHub, Google, Formspree, Mailchimp and Stripe) are based in, or transfer data to, the USA and other countries outside the EU/EEA. Where that is the case, transfers are safeguarded by appropriate mechanisms such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Because we work with clients and artisans both inside and outside the EU, personal data you share with us may also be processed in Tunisia for the sole purpose of delivering a booked experience.

Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purpose it was collected and for any statutory retention periods (for example, accounting records under Bulgarian law). Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

  • Access, rectification and erasure of your data
  • Restriction of, and objection to, processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time (e.g. newsletter), without affecting prior processing
  • To lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these, email crafted.visions@outlook.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Bulgarian supervisory authority, the Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP), 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., 1592 Sofia, Bulgaria (cpdp.bg), or with the data-protection authority in your country of residence.
